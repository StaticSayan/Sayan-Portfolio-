Selected projects

03

Covariate-Adjusted Response-Adaptive Design in Clinical Trials (Dissertation)

Kernel smoothing · Adaptive design
  • Built a covariate-adjusted response-adaptive allocation algorithm for sequential clinical trials, using kernel smoothing to predict patient-specific outcomes from historical treatment efficacy.
  • Formulated time-adjusted estimators to correct for random observation delays, reducing estimation bias toward fast responders under fixed-duration constraints.
  • Designed a time-decaying probabilistic assignment model balancing ethical patient advantage against statistical balance via dynamic tuning constants.

Bird Species Diversity & Distribution Analysis

Python · Power BI
  • Unified 50,000+ bird observations from 10+ national park units to compare biodiversity across forest and grassland ecosystems.
  • Identified 5+ biodiversity hotspots and 20+ at-risk species, informing conservation, eco-tourism and habitat-restoration recommendations.
  • Assessed four environmental variables and human disturbance to isolate the key ecological drivers.

Amazon Prime Video (US) Content Insights

Python · Pandas
  • Analysed 9,000+ streaming titles to surface genre dominance, yearly growth patterns and contributor concentration.
  • Evaluated regional distribution and IMDb performance to highlight catalog acquisition and diversification opportunities.

Uber Supply–Demand Gap Analysis

Python · Excel
  • Identified bimodal demand peaks (5–10 AM, 5–9 PM) and 40%+ "no cars available" cases in evenings, concentrated at airport pickups.
  • Recommended driver reallocation and surge forecasting targeting a ~20% cut in unfulfilled trips.

Student Extracurricular Activities (ECA) Survey Analysis

R · Excel · Survey design
